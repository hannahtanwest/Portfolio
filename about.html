About

© Hannah West | 2026

Hi, My Name Is Hannah!

I'm a Copenhagen-based digital creative working across design, technology, branding and content.

My background is in Multimedia Design and Web Development, which is where I developed my interest in creating digital experiences that bring together strong visuals and functionality. Over time, that interest has grown beyond the screen and into branding, content, photography, video and experience-driven projects.

Alongside my work in digital design and development, I founded Amentes, a Copenhagen-based queer event and community brand. What started as an idea for creating the kinds of spaces I felt were missing has grown into a project where I handle everything from brand direction, visual identities and marketing campaigns to partnerships, budgets and event production. Building Amentes has taught me how to take an idea from its earliest stage and turn it into something people can actually see, engage with and experience.

I also have experience creating photography, video and social content for artists and smaller brands, which has strengthened my interest in visual storytelling and the way brands communicate across different formats.

I enjoy working where creative thinking and practical problem-solving meet. Whether I'm designing an interface, developing a campaign, creating content or building an event concept, I'm interested in understanding who it's for, what it should communicate and how all the individual pieces come together into one experience.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me playing guitar or piano, cooking for the people around me, taking photos or starting another creative project I probably didn't need to start.
